## 2026-09-30: chaos realigns into order

USER-STATED, quoted from a working session, field sentences only:

> "Chaos realigns into order, this has to do with atemporality, also
> hyperorder and hyperchaos theories might need grounding from hyperethics,
> Im not sure"

This statement is `REALIGNMENT` in [hyperorder.py](hyperorder.py), with
status OPEN. It names order and hyperorder separately, and it does not say
that the order chaos realigns into is hyperorder. That is Q1.

Later the same day he named hyperorder, with hyperchaos and hyperreality, as
fields the family was missing, and asked for a repository for each of
hyperorder, hyperchaos and hyperemergence. This repository is that request.

## 2026-09-30: the five verdicts

ASSISTANT-PROPOSED. Two assistants worked through the 2026-09-30 statement
in a read-only research exchange. Among their corrections:
- a missing relation between two events is UNKNOWN, not concurrent;
- the verdicts are five: before, after, concurrent, unknown and conflicted;
- "after" equals "not before" only in a strict total order.

This field keeps that discipline and renames the verdicts atemporally:
PRECEDES, FOLLOWS, INCOMPARABLE, UNKNOWN and CONFLICTED. The same exchange
recorded two limits, which this field keeps:
- Hyperorder keeps its dichrome meaning.
- An operational target, such as a set of states a process settles into, is
  not thereby hyperorder.
