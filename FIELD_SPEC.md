## The ordinal bookkeeping

[FRAME] Hypergrammar's `TIME.md` row 10 says "space" and "dimensionality"
infer numerics, not ordinality. The frame takes the minimum that follows for
a finite claim about order:

- An `Order` has 1 to 64 named positions and at most 256 declarations.
  A position is a non-empty name. A number, including a Boolean, is refused.
- `Precedes(a, b)` and `Incomparable(a, b)` are declarations about two
  distinct declared positions.
- `compare` returns one of five verdicts:
  - PRECEDES or FOLLOWS when a chain of declarations connects the two;
  - INCOMPARABLE only when that was declared;
  - UNKNOWN when nothing was declared either way;
  - CONFLICTED when the declarations contradict each other, as in a cycle,
    or a declared incomparable pair that a chain connects.
- FOLLOWS is not "does not precede". The two coincide only when the order is
  declared total, and `total=True` is checked: every pair of distinct
  positions must be connected, and no pair may be declared incomparable.

The five verdicts and "a missing relation is UNKNOWN" come from a 2026-09-30
research exchange on chaos and order; see [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).
They are bookkeeping that any claim about hyperorder would have to pass.
They are not hyperorder.
